Free the otters from the pond

A cozy puzzle you play with one finger. Each otter swims straight ahead, the way its head is pointing: find the right order.

Coming soon to Google Play

How to play

Tap an otter: if nothing blocks its way, it swims out of the pond and into your net. Three otters of the same color in the net? They leave together and make room. But if the net overflows, the round is lost. Think about the order, clear the paths, and empty the pond.

Calm, one-handed

  • One gesture: tap.
  • No timer, no pressure.
  • Short rounds, perfect for a break.

200 levels and bonuses

  • A map that travels up the river, through nine landscapes.
  • Three stars to earn in every level.
  • Bonus branches to explore.

Daily challenge

  • Three grids every day: easy, medium and hard.

Competition mode

A new idea in every chapter

Mystery otters, melting ice, cages and keys, whirlpools, twins that leave together, currents that sweep everything along. The otter holding a key opens the cage with its letter.

An otter keeps you company

It explains each new idea at the right moment, and reacts to how you play.
